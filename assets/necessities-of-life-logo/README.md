# Necessities of Life — Logo concept 01

The mark combines three ideas:

- a lowercase-friendly **N** monogram for “necessities”
- connected **AI/data nodes** along the diagonal
- a minimal **leaf** at the endpoint, representing useful technology that improves life

## Palette

- Ink: `#0B1020`
- Life lime: `#C9FF4A`
- Warm white: `#F7F5EE`
- Slate: `#657083`

## Files

- `necessities-of-life-primary.svg` — primary horizontal lockup
- `necessities-of-life-icon.svg` — profile/avatar icon
- `necessities-of-life-monochrome.svg` — one-color fallback
- `necessities-of-life-preview.svg` — presentation board

The SVG wordmark uses `Inter` with system fallbacks. Convert the final selected wordmark to outlines before sending to a print vendor.
