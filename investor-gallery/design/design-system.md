# LONG VIEW design system

## Direction

An editorial investor archive inspired by a private research library and museum catalogues. The page is restrained, image-led, and avoids dashboard cards or decorative finance clichés.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#08131A`
- Ink raised: `#0B1820`
- Paper: `#F2EEE6`
- Muted paper: `#9DA7A9`
- Oxidized brass: `#B58B48`
- Brass light: `#C5A368`
- Oxblood atmosphere: `#74292C`
- Hairline: `rgba(237, 232, 221, 0.13)`

## Typography

- Display: `Noto Serif SC`, `Songti SC`, Georgia, serif
- Body: `Noto Sans SC`, `PingFang SC`, sans-serif
- Utility: Inter-like sans serif, uppercase, generous tracking

## Layout

- Quiet 88px header on desktop.
- Split hero: short thesis on the left, one archival portrait on the right.
- Open asymmetric mosaic rather than repeated cards.
- Desktop max width: 1440px; primary gutters: 64px.
- Mobile collapses the hero and uses a two-column editorial grid.

## Signature

A thin brass "compounding trajectory" passes behind the portraits and shifts subtly with scroll. It represents time and accumulated judgment; all other motion stays restrained.

## Interaction

- Filters switch between investment schools.
- Portrait hover reveals a one-sentence thesis.
- Clicking a portrait opens a focused detail drawer.
- Escape and backdrop click close the drawer; focus remains visible.
- Reduced-motion mode disables scroll and reveal motion.
